  - `metrics.ts`: `matrixAt(scores: readonly number[], labels: readonly (0 | 1)[], threshold: number): Confusion` ve `rowMetrics(c: Confusion): { precision: number | null; recall: number | null; f1: number; accuracy: number }` (F1 tanımsız kesinlikte 0); `Confusion` `degerlendirme/metrics.ts`'ten.
